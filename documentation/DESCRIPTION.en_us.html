Tracet is a family of handwriting-model typefaces for learning to read and write, designed by David Martínez at Paraula.

Tracet Pal is an upright print-script model, the first letterforms children learn to write. It comes in four weights, from Thin to ExtraBold. Tracet Pal Dotted draws the same letters as dotted lines, for tracing practice in worksheets and notebooks.

All the letters are built from one stroke skeleton, the same line a child follows with a pencil. That keeps the shapes simple and consistent across weights and between the solid and dotted styles.

To contribute, see github.com/paraula/tracet.
