Tracet is a family of handwriting-model typefaces for learning to read and write, designed by David Martínez at Paraula.

Tracet Lligada is the joined (cursive) model, the handwriting children move on to once they know the print letters of Tracet Pal. Every letter connects to the next one with the same stroke a child makes with a pencil. The joins are handled by OpenType rules: letters after o, b, v and w enter high, and accented letters keep their joins. It comes in Regular and Bold.

Tracet Lligada Dotted draws the same joined letters as dotted lines, for tracing practice in worksheets and notebooks.

To contribute, see github.com/paraula/tracet.
